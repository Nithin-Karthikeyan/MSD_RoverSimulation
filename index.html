MODEL USED

Control equations

Drive position error: ep = ‖pgoal − prover‖

vref = sat(Kpep + Ki∫epdt + Kddep/dt)

dv/dt = (vref − v)/τv, dx/dt = v cos ψ, dy/dt = v sin ψ

Steering: eθ = θref − θ

uθ = sat(Kpeθ + Ki∫eθdt + Kddeθ/dt)

dω/dt = (1.65uθ − ω)/0.115, dθ/dt = ω

Wheel angle is physically constrained to ±90°. Crab is exactly +90°, so it cannot overshoot without violating the mechanical stop. Use Bishop (45°) to observe an interior-step response.
